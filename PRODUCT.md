# Product

## Platform

Web (mobile first). English and Hindi.

## Vertical

**Families and housing communities in Delhi-NCR facing severe winter smog.** The challenge statement
names no list of verticals, so Saans takes the two audiences it names: _families_ and _communities_.
A household chooses "my family" or "a housing society or community" and gets advice at that scale.

## Users

- **A family:** parents, children, grandparents, often on a phone, often more comfortable in Hindi. They
  want to know what to do today, not what the AQI is.
- **A resident welfare association or society committee:** wants actions that serve many households,
  like a shared clean-air room, a carpool roster and a no-burning pledge.

## Purpose

Delhi-NCR's winter smog is a daily decision problem. Saans reads today's air for the person's own
neighbourhood and builds a day for their own household: a low-emission routine, indoor air hacks and
greener travel. Every suggestion states its direct, computed impact, either how much less smog the
household breathes or how much carbon it avoids.

## Principles

1. **Code decides, AI explains.** Exposure, percentages and CO2 come from tested code. The model writes
   words and proposes changes; it never produces a number.
2. **Show the impact, honestly.** Individual action cannot clean a city's air, but it does change what a
   household breathes. The app says so, shows its assumptions, and never trades more smog for less carbon.
3. **The person decides.** The assistant proposes; nothing changes until it is approved.
4. **Works without AI, without sign-in, on a slow phone.** The computed plan arrives without waiting for
   a model, and sign-in only adds sync.
5. **No medical advice.** General guidance only, with a clear pointer to a doctor.

## Accessibility and inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA audited by axe in CI on desktop and phone, in English and Hindi. Colour is never the only
signal. Touch targets are at least 44px. Correct `lang` on Hindi text. Reduced motion respected.

## Evidence on hand

No real users, testimonials or usage numbers exist. None are claimed. Plans shown before a household is
entered are labelled as examples.
